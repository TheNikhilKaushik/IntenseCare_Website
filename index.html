NDIS Support Provider

IntenseCare is a NDIS (National Disability Insurance Scheme) support provider, providing disability services in South Australia to all NDIS participants.

We ensure that all of your care needs are satisfied by providing compassionate, skilled, and committed care. Our company was built on the principles of our enthusiasm for helping those in need.
