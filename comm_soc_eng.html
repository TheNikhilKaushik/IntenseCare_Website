Connect, participate and belong—our Community & Social Engagement service links you with welcoming groups, events and friendships that lift wellbeing and fight isolation.

What is Community & Social Engagement

It's hands-on support that gets you out, involved, and making real connections in your community.

What do I get?

Everything you need to join in confidently—personal planning, transport, social events, skills coaching, and regular progress checks.

  • Personalised Activity Planning  - outings and events matched to your interests.
  • Transport and Accompaniment  - we get you there and stay for support.
  • Small-group Socials  - coffee catch-ups, hobby clubs, or sport days to meet new mates.
  • Skills for Community Living  - guidance on using public facilities, money handling, or travel cards.
  • Regular Progress Check-ins  - we review goals together to keep every outing meaningful.
